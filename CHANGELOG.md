# Changelog

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-15

- The portal player now opens a compact campaign menu instead of a second full-size website.
- Embedded farm controls and objectives fit inside the player, with a clear playfield and accessible animal and market panels.
- Storage-restricted iframes now remain playable instead of opening a blank screen. Existing saves and standalone play are preserved; this update begins tracked releases.
