# Happy Farm Rush

## Pitch
A twenty-day farm time-management campaign. Feed animals, collect products, manufacture goods and trade while protecting the farm.
## Core gameplay loop
Plant grass, feed autonomous animals, collect, process, ship, defend and complete objectives for medals and persistent upgrades.
## Game rules
Existing configured economy, hunger, storage, timers and bear schedules remain authoritative. See ../GAME_DESIGN.md and ../BALANCE.md.
## Win / lose conditions
Complete each day's explicit goals. Time affects medals, not a hard loss.
## Art style
Original SVG storybook farm, cream, sage and terracotta.
## Audio direction
Owner-supplied Breakfast in the Orchard and generated effects; music continues through menus, hidden tabs pause.
## Player goals
Finish the campaign and improve medals.
## Anti-goals
The embedded-layout update does not redesign gameplay, migrate saves or replace the engine.
## References
Classic Farm Frenzy mechanics, using original artwork.
## Open questions
Owner visual playtest of the compact portal presentation.
